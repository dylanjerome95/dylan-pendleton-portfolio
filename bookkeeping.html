FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you prepare or file taxes?

No — I’m not a tax professional. But your books will be organized, categorized, and ready, so tax time is smooth for whoever prepares your return.

What do you need from me to get started?

Typically: access to your bank and credit card statements (read-only access works), a list of your customers and vendors, and any existing records. We’ll sort out the details on our intro call.

How do we share financial documents?

Securely and digitally. During onboarding we’ll agree on a simple, private way to exchange statements and receipts — your information stays confidential, always.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your transaction volume and how much cleanup is needed. Request a consultation and I’ll give you a clear, honest quote — no surprises.

My books are a mess. Can you still help?

Absolutely — that’s exactly what bookkeeping cleanup is for. We’ll organize what you have, correct discrepancies, and get you to a clean starting point.
